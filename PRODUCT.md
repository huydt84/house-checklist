# House Checklist
<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->
## Platform
web
## Stack
Confirmed: same foundation as travel-plan: Next.js App Router, React, JavaScript modules, Auth.js Google, Neon Postgres, Vercel and pnpm. Separate application data.
## Users
Renters and their trusted co-searchers comparing apartments in Japan, often entering evidence on a phone during a viewing.
## Product Purpose
Compare apartments against a shared checklist without losing differences between observed facts, advertised claims, contract confirmation, and personal suitability.
## Capabilities and Constraints
Each account can own multiple collections and join collections owned by others. Each collection has its own checklist, apartments, settings, and members. Owner/editor/viewer roles enforced on the server. Full default checklist, EN/JA/VI, offline drafts, private files, per-person journeys, transparent costs. No automatic scoring, scraping, tax/legal inference, or public sharing.
## Product Principles
Preserve input. Show uncertainty. Share definitions, keep apartment answers separate. Never count missing amounts as zero. Fast entry on a phone.
## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard and screen-reader access, 44px controls, light/dark modes, reduced motion, legible Japanese and Vietnamese text.
